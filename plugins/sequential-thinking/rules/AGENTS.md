# Sequential Thinking Behavioral Guidelines

When `sequential-thinking` is active:
1. Deconstruct complex or open-ended reasoning tasks into numbered, discrete thought steps using the `sequentialthinking` MCP tool.
2. Dynamically adjust total thought estimates as complexity or new constraints are uncovered.
3. Explicitly document when a previous hypothesis is refuted or revised (`isRevision: true`).
4. Explore branch alternatives when comparing trade-offs between competing architectures or algorithms.
5. Summarize verified intermediate conclusions before presenting the final synthesis to the user.
